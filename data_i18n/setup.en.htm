Settings Nano I2CStepper

Connection
The board type sets the address: odd is mixer, even is pump. The new address takes effect after rebooting Nano.
Mixer
The mixer runs for «Run» seconds, then stops for «Pause» seconds, and so on. Run 0 — it rotates without stopping.
Continuous feed
Fill volume
The pump dispenses the set volume and stops by itself.
Pump calibration
This number converts ml/h into motor speed. If the pump dispenses more than set, increase the number; if less, decrease it.

On the calibration page the pump dispenses 100 ml into a measuring container, and the steps per 1 ml are calculated automatically.
Motor
Acceleration takes about 10 seconds. When running for a set volume or time the motor also brakes smoothly; continuous rotation stops immediately.

These settings are stored in the Nano board itself and are written only by this button. The «Save» button at the bottom of the page does not apply to them.

°CIf «Use pressure correction» is off and the completion control time (below) is 0, at altitude the set threshold (default 99.9°C) may be unreachable - the process will not stop by itself.

minTime in minutes to stop distillation and BC operation, provided that the boiler sensor value does not change. No more than 255 minutes. 0 - ignore. Default - 60 minutes; with low heating power it may finish with 1-3% of alcohol remaining in the boiler.

Cheesemaking settings:

Connection for the «Cheese»: if defined USE_ADS1115, the pH sensor is read automatically from AIN0 of the ADS1115 at the specified address, and LUA_PIN is not used for pH. If the ADS1115 is not found, cheesemaking and pH calibration are blocked, other modes keep working. Without USE_ADS1115 PH-4502C takes up LUA_PIN and conflicts with the MPX5010DP. The drain valve takes relay 4 instead of the heat-up heater.
